Only the row is read, never the prose, so a passing mention does not count. Worlds the guide never visits are left out. {"worlds": {"Tatooine": {"slug": "tatooine", "count": 81, "entries": [{"t": title, "u": url, "c": category, "j": journal id}, ...]}, ...}} -#} {% macro
